Dugnad: working together for a common goal

“Dugnad” means people joining hands to get something done. At our school that spirit shapes every classroom: pupils, parents and teachers share the work, and children learn through activities they can see, touch and finish.

Our mission is to raise confident, capable learners through activity-based teaching in a green, safe environment.

Integrity

Academics that start with doing

A blended curriculum, a computer lab and clubs that let children try, practise and show what they know.

Learning that blends subjects and activities

Classroom lessons are paired with projects, group tasks and hands-on practice from Daycare through Primary, so concepts in reading, numbers and science are applied, not just memorised.

Computer learning lab

Pupils use educational apps and programming games designed for children, building comfort with computers and early logical thinking in a guided setting.
